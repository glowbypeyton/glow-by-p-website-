Your Esthetician

Hi, I'm Peyton

I'm the esthetician behind Glow by P, and I'm passionate about holistic skincare—the kind that looks at your skin as part of the whole picture, not just something to fix. I believe skincare should be personalized, which is why I love taking the time to really understand your skin, your habits, and what will actually work for your life.

I focus on natural, intentional treatments and I care a lot about using non-toxic products—both in the studio and in what I recommend for home. More than anything, I want every client to leave feeling comfortable, cared for, and confident in their own skin.

My Approach

Skin, beyond the surface.

At Glow by P, I look at skin beyond just surface-level concerns. Everyday habits—stress, sleep, hydration, your skincare routine, and lifestyle in general—can all affect the way your skin looks and feels. My goal is to help you understand those connections so we can build a routine and treatment plan that actually makes sense for you.
